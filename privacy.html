Privacy & Cookie Policy

This policy explains what personal data Sovereignty Global collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Please review before publishing. This document reflects how the website and client portal actually process data, but the controller’s registered legal name, company registration number and registered address still need to be inserted below, and the final text should be checked by a qualified data-protection adviser.

1. Who we are

Sovereignty Global (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides residency, tax and banking structuring services focused on Bulgaria and Georgia. For the purposes of data-protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

[Registered entity name], [registration number], [registered address]. You can contact us about privacy at legal@sovereigntyglobal.org.

2. The data we collect

Visitors to this website

You can browse the public site without giving us any personal data. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider processes standard technical information (such as your IP address and request metadata) to deliver and secure the site.

People who book a consultation

Consultations are scheduled through Calendly. When you book, Calendly collects the details you provide (such as your name, email address and any notes) and shares them with us so we can prepare for and hold the meeting.

Clients using the secure portal

When we create a client account, and as we work together, we process:

  • Account details — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile details — where relevant to your matter: phone number, nationality, country of residence, tax residence, address and an internal client reference.
  • Documents — files we exchange with you (for example residency, tax and banking paperwork), stored as encrypted-at-rest objects.
  • Service records — document requests, their status, and security and activity logs needed to operate the portal safely.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide our services and manage your account and documents — performance of a contract.
  • To secure the portal, prevent abuse, rate-limit sign-in attempts and keep audit records — our legitimate interests in protecting the service and our clients.
  • To respond to enquiries and schedule consultations — legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including record-keeping and anti-fraud requirements — legal obligation.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not sell your data.

4. Cookies and local storage

We keep this deliberately minimal. We do not use advertising, profiling or third-party analytics cookies.

5. Fonts and third parties

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. To serve them, Google may receive your IP address. We use the following processors and service providers, who act on our instructions:

  • Cloudflare — website and application hosting, content delivery, database and file storage, and security.
  • Google — delivery of web fonts.
  • Calendly — consultation scheduling.

6. International transfers

Delivering our services and coordinating banking may involve transferring personal data outside the European Economic Area — for example to Georgia, and to service providers that operate globally. Where we do this, we rely on an adequacy decision or on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it. Our systems automatically clear operational records on a routine schedule, including:

  • Expired portal sessions — removed once expired.
  • Failed sign-in records — removed after 24 hours.
  • Unused invitations — removed 30 days after they expire.
  • Security and activity events — removed after 12 months.
  • Sent operational notifications — removed after 90 days.

Client account details and documents are kept for the life of the engagement and for as long afterwards as we are required to for legal, regulatory or legitimate business reasons, after which they are deleted.

8. Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the law, you have the right to: access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict or object to our processing; receive a copy in a portable format; and withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise any of these, email legal@sovereigntyglobal.org.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to your local supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP).

9. Keeping data secure

Passwords are stored using salted PBKDF2 hashing, sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies over HTTPS, uploads are validated and stored with restricted access, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. Staff access is protected by an identity provider.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “last updated” date above and, where changes are significant, take reasonable steps to let affected clients know.
