Bulgaria

A 10% tax base inside the EU.

Bulgaria combines a 10% flat personal income tax rate and 10% corporate income tax rate with EU membership, relatively low operating costs and a practical base for people who earn internationally.

Assess your Bulgaria fitHow it works

We do not sell a tax rate. We build the structure behind it.

A Bulgarian address or company alone does not automatically make your worldwide position work. Tax residency, income source, company management, social-security exposure, banking, substance and your ties to other countries all matter. We coordinate those pieces before you move.

10%

Personal income tax

Bulgaria applies a flat 10% personal income tax rate. Whether that rate applies to your income depends on your tax residence, income type and circumstances.

10%

Corporate income tax

Bulgarian companies are generally subject to a 10% corporate income tax rate, making Bulgaria one of the EU’s most competitive mainstream business jurisdictions.

EU

A European operating base

For the right client, Bulgaria can combine tax efficiency with EU legal infrastructure, local banking, accounting and a real place to live and operate.

Who Bulgaria can work well for.

We focus on people whose income and lifestyle can genuinely support an international move: consultants, founders, agency owners, freelancers, developers, creators, traders, ecommerce operators and remote professionals.

  • People leaving higher-tax countries and willing to establish genuine Bulgarian residence
  • Owner-managed businesses that can align management and operations with Bulgaria
  • International earners who want a straightforward EU base rather than an offshore structure
  • Individuals who need residency, company, banking and accounting coordinated together
