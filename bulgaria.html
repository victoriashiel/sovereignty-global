Why people choose Bulgaria.

Low tax, EU membership and a straightforward company environment make Bulgaria attractive for people who earn internationally.

10%

Personal income tax

A flat 10% rate for Bulgarian tax residents, subject to the normal rules for residence and income type.

10%

Corporate income tax

A 10% corporate rate for Bulgarian companies, with the usual requirements around management and activity.

EU

Live in Bulgaria, travel across Europe and keep your business within an EU jurisdiction.
