Documents

Preparing your file

Good onboarding starts before the bank, adviser or authority asks the first question.

SG guide4 minute read

In this guide

IdentityAddressFundsCompany documents

A clear file is easier to approve than a large file.

Identity

Keep passports, civil documents and any required certified copies current. Names and dates should match across the file.

Address

Banks and authorities often ask for recent proof of address. Make sure the address is written consistently and the document is recent enough for the institution using it.

Source of funds

Be ready to show where the money for a transaction came from. Salary, dividends, a property sale, investment proceeds or a business exit should be supported by documents that connect clearly.

Company documents

For business banking, expect questions about ownership, activity, clients, suppliers, expected turnover and payment countries.

Takeaway

The reviewer should not have to guess how your story fits together.
