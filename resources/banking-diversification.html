Banking across borders

One good account may be enough until your life, business or tax residence moves across countries.

SG guide4 minute read

In this guide

Why diversifyWhy GeorgiaPrepare the fileChoose the right bank

International banking should make your life easier, not add more accounts for the sake of it.

Why diversify

If all of your money and payments sit with one bank, a compliance review can disrupt everything at once. A second relationship can reduce that risk.

It can also help with currencies, business payments and access when you live or operate across more than one country.

Why Georgia

For suitable clients, Georgia can provide useful personal or business banking outside the EU. We arrange applications and onboarding alongside Bulgarian residency or company structures where it makes sense.

Account opening is never guaranteed. The bank makes the final decision.

Prepare the file first

Banks will want to understand who you are, where you live, what your company does and where the money comes from. A clean KYC and source of funds file makes the process easier.

Choose the bank for the client

The right institution depends on nationality, business activity, expected payments and currencies. We match the route to the profile instead of sending the same application everywhere.

Takeaway

A second bank account is useful when it solves a real problem.
